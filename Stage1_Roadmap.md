# Stage 1 Project Roadmap

Group plan for the DATA1x02 Stage 1 project, prepared from the unit's Stage 1
specification and the Stage 1 slides. It sets out what is due, when it is due, and
who is responsible, so that all members work to the same expectations.

## Project question

**How well does the index represent the market?** The investigation covers index
concentration, survivorship bias in index membership, and constituent turnover.
Confirmed 12 September 2026.

## What each member provides

- One dataset of their own that contributes evidence to the project question, with a
  minimum of 10 attributes and 100 records in its cleaned form.
- The raw dataset as obtained, together with the cleaned dataset.
- Python code for cleaning and quality checking, and Python code for the summary
  analysis, with the outputs included in the report.
- Two to three charts, and no more than three, produced from the cleaned dataset,
  each accompanied by a written evaluation of how the data are encoded and of the
  design's strengths and limitations.
- Report contributions: 2 pages for section B and 4 pages for section C.

Dataset sourcing and allocation will be confirmed in a separate dataset plan, so
that members' datasets contribute distinct evidence rather than overlapping.

## Group deliverables

- **Section A, Introduction** (1 page): the question, why it matters, the
  stakeholders and how they benefit, and a description of the data obtained.
- **Section D, Group Findings, Recommendations and Responsible AI Collaboration**
  (2 pages): the evidence synthesised into a single project narrative, the
  recommendations, the limitations, and an account of how AI assistance was used,
  verified and challenged.
- **Cover page** identifying the group and its members.
- **AI Assisted Decision** (maximum 150 words): one occasion where AI influenced the
  project, whether its suggestion was accepted or rejected, and the justification
  for the final decision.
- **Submission package**: one compressed file containing the combined report as a
  PDF, and a folder holding a subfolder per member with their code, raw data and
  cleaned data. Only one member needs to submit the package.

## Responsible use of AI

The unit permits and encourages generative AI as a collaborator for coding,
documentation and communication, on the condition that outputs are verified and the
final analytical decisions remain the group's. Each important AI assisted output
should be checked against the data or an independent source before it is relied
upon. The report must describe how this was done, so keep a running record of what
AI was used for, what was verified, and where a suggestion was rejected.
